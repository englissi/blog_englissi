주제: 재택근무 vs 사무실 출근
Do you prefer working from home or at the office?
P
결론 (Point)
I totally prefer working from home.
R
핵심 이유 (Reason)
It saves so much energy. Commuting is exhausting.
E
구체적 사례/장면 (Example)
Yesterday it poured rain. I just worked with my warm coffee.
P
마무리 (Point)
So yeah, I'm way more productive this way.
